AI Driver teaches itself to drive the current track by trial and error.

It starts out mashing buttons at random. Every attempt earns reward for getting closer to the next checkpoint, for passing checkpoints and for finishing. The best attempts pass on their "brain" (a tiny neural network) to the next generation, and the random mashing fades as it improves.

K toggles the AI. Shift+K forgets what it learned on this track.

AI-driven runs are blocked from the online leaderboard, and this mod marks the game as modded for multiplayer.
